    Study area

    Mirpur Khas is a city and the district headquarters of Mirpur Khas District in south-eastern Sindh, Pakistan. It is a trading centre for the surrounding agricultural area and is well known for its mangoes. This map shows the bank branches and ATMs in and around the city.

    Data and method

    Locations were collected from Google Maps and OpenStreetMap and stored as point features in a PostgreSQL/PostGIS database. A Node.js (Express) API reads them from the database and sends them to this Leaflet map as GeoJSON.
